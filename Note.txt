Bids: bid_id, seller_email, listing_id, bidder_email, bid_price
Categories: parent_category, category_name
Credit_Cards: credit_card_num, card_type, expire_month, expire_year, security_code, owner_email

Transactions: transaction_id, seller_email, listing_id, bidder_email, date, payment
Users: email, password
Zipcode_Info: zipcode, city, state

Primary Keys
Address: address ID
Auction_Listings: Seller Email, Listing ID
Bidders: email
Bids: Bid ID
Categories: category name
Credit_Cards: credit card num
Helpdesk: email
Local_Vendors: Email
Rating: Bidder Email, Seller Email, Date
Requests: request id
Sellers: email
Transactions: Transaction ID
Users: email
Zipcode_Info: zipcode

Foreign Keys
Auction_Listings: Seller Email 引用 Sellers(email)
Auction_Listings: Category 引用 Categories(category name)
Bidders: email 引用 Users(email)
Bidders: home_address_id 引用 Address(address ID)
Bids: (Seller_Email, Listing_ID) 复合引用 Auction_Listings(Seller Email, Listing ID)
Bids: Bidder_email 引用 Bidders(email)
Categories: parent_category 引用 Categories(category name)
Credit_Cards: Owner_email 引用 Bidders(email)
Helpdesk: email 引用 Users(email)
Local_Vendors: Email 引用 Sellers(email)
Local_Vendors: Business_Address_ID 引用 Address(address ID)
Rating: Bidder Email 引用 Bidders(email)
Rating: Seller Email 引用 Sellers(email)
Requests: sender_email 引用 Users(email)
Requests: helpdesk_staff_email 引用 Helpdesk(email)
Sellers: email 引用 Users(email)
Transactions: (Seller_Email, Listing_ID) 复合引用 Auction_Listings(Seller Email, Listing ID)
Transactions: Buyer_Email 引用 Bidders(email)
